About AAI

The most loved and iconic brand, AAI, the first of its kind molecular gastronomy concept club from NAGPUR is now open in Maharashtra. The largest floor space for any club in NAGPUR. AAI is a unique concept of Roti, Brewery and Kitchen redefining the era of World War 2.
The wide range of delicious delectable, expansive North Indian, Italian,Chinese and Continental Cuisines and unique cocktails makes AAI one of the best Air Bar and Kitchen in tricity. The niche of AAI is based on the combination of gastronomical recipes and innovative dishes. Also, the quirky ambiance and a vivacious seating add more to the lounging experience.
Get ready to witness the affordable and distinctive getaway from your daily hustles, AAI is coming to NAGPUR to reshape your usual dining experience.
